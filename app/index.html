Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no online trophies. No mission replay and no New Game Plus — keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots, 1 autosave). Leave cheats off: they switch off trophies and saving.
