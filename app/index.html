Method & sources

Names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags are the official PlayStation 5 list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles (the two agree on all 55), cross-checked for the hidden/visible split against Planet GTA 6 and GTA Zone. The roadmap and the 100% completion breakdown follow PowerPyx: platinum difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, 55 offline / 0 online, one playthrough, five story chapters of 60–80 hours. Every guide on this page is written in our own words for this guide; each trophy names its sources beside it, and where no verified method exists yet — five trophies, marked in their guides — we say so rather than inventing steps.
